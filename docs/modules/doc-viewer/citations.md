# Citation guide

These are bibliographic links, not redistributed papers or evidence that the simulator agrees with a measured device. This curated selection is not the entire repository bibliography. Citation text is repository-authored; publisher figures and PDFs are not shipped.

## Hysteresis and material models

- Materlik, Kuenneth and Kersch (2015), *The origin of ferroelectricity in Hf1-xZrxO2: A computational investigation and a surface energy model*, Journal of Applied Physics 117, 134109. [DOI: 10.1063/1.4916229](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4916229). The repository uses thermodynamic simulation anchors, not a measured HZO thin-film fit.
- Park et al. (2015), *Ferroelectricity and Antiferroelectricity of Doped Thin HfO2-Based Films*, Advanced Materials 27, 1811–1831. [DOI: 10.1002/adma.201404531](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.201404531). See the [correction](../../research/validation/park-reference-correction.md) before interpreting any Park-context comparison.
- Alessandri et al. (2018), *Switching Dynamics of Ferroelectric HZO*, IEEE Electron Device Letters 39, 1780–1783. [DOI: 10.1109/LED.2018.2872124](https://doi.org/10.1109/LED.2018.2872124). Bibliographic metadata follows the repository bibliography; the link is not a new verification receipt.
- Guo et al. (2018), *Nucleation-limited polarization switching in ferroelectric HfO2 capacitors*, Applied Physics Letters 112, 262903. [DOI: 10.1063/1.5038038](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5038038).
